Teams do not forget because they lack documents. They forget because decisions are buried across docs, meeting notes, transcripts, and launch checklists.

Debrief recovers a cited Decision Brief from scattered team context: what was decided, why, who owns it, what is risky or contradictory, and what is still unresolved.

Here is the live demo. Sign in with the Build Week demo account, then open the Launch Planning workspace. Five sample docs are already uploaded and ready.

Generate the Decision Brief. Debrief recovers key decisions with rationale and owners, budget in rupees, assumptions, metrics, risks, open questions, and date conflicts — each with source citations. This is the first pass a team needs before a launch review — not a generic summary.

Ask a follow-up: what did we decide about pricing and why? Answers stream with citations, excerpts, and relevance scores so you can verify the claim in the original docs.

Under the hood, Debrief uses React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL with pgvector, and OpenRouter free models for embeddings, decision briefs, and cited streaming chat.

I built this during OpenAI Build Week with Codex powered by GPT-5.6.
