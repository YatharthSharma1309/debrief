# Customer Feedback Summary

Feedback window: July 1-11, 2026

Top themes:
- Small teams liked the fast workspace setup.
- Users asked for clearer pricing before inviting teammates.
- Several beta customers wanted examples of launch checklists.
- Two larger customers asked about SSO and invoice billing.

Decision signal:
Self-serve Pro is a better launch fit than enterprise sales because most interested beta users are teams with fewer than 20 people.

Risk:
The product may look too lightweight for enterprise buyers until admin controls and SSO are ready.

Open question:
Should marketing mention enterprise features as "coming soon" or avoid them completely during launch?
