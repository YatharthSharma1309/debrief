# Product Requirements - Atlas Launch

Project: Atlas self-serve launch
Owner: Maya Patel
Target users: small product teams that need a lightweight planning workspace.

Key requirements:
- Launch a self-serve Pro plan for beta customers.
- Keep onboarding under 10 minutes.
- Include import support for meeting notes and project documents.
- Provide a launch checklist before public announcement.

Metric targets:
- Onboarding completion under 10 minutes (owner: Maya)
- Activation: at least 60% of new Pro trials create a second document in week 1

Assumptions:
- Beta users are mostly small teams and founders who need speed more than procurement workflows.
- India public prices will be listed only in rupees (₹ / INR).

Decision (approved):
The team agreed to prioritize self-serve onboarding before enterprise admin controls.

Decision (deferred):
Enterprise SSO and admin controls are deferred until after the first public launch.

Open question:
Enterprise SSO remains unresolved and should not block the first public launch.

Risk:
If onboarding takes longer than 10 minutes, the launch may miss the activation target.
