# Pricing Notes - Atlas Launch

Meeting date: July 9, 2026
Participants: Maya, Arjun, Lena, Sofia

Pricing decision:
The team decided to launch the Pro plan at $29 per user per month.

Rationale:
- Sales-led pricing created too much friction for beta users.
- The $29 price is low enough for small teams to try without procurement.
- Annual discounts will be deferred until after the first launch cycle.

Owner:
Arjun owns the pricing page copy and checkout test.

Risk:
Enterprise packaging is still undefined. Lena noted that larger customers may ask for SSO, admin roles, and invoice billing.

Open question:
Should the free plan include 3 projects or 5 projects?
