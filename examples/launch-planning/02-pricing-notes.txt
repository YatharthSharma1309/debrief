# Pricing Notes - Atlas Launch

Meeting date: July 9, 2026
Participants: Maya, Arjun, Lena, Sofia
Currency: Indian Rupees (₹ / INR)

Approved launch pricing (INR):
- Free: ₹0 — limited projects (count still open)
- Pro: ₹2,499 per user per month
- Team: ₹6,499 per user per month
- Enterprise: custom quote after launch (SSO, admin roles, invoice billing)

Pricing decision:
The team decided to launch self-serve Pro at ₹2,499 per user per month (about $29 USD at planning FX). Team plan launches at ₹6,499 per user per month. No USD-only catalog for the India launch; all public prices show in rupees.

Rationale:
- Sales-led pricing created too much friction for beta users.
- ₹2,499 is low enough for small Indian teams to try without procurement.
- Annual discounts and multi-year contracts are deferred until after the first launch cycle.
- Showing prices in ₹ avoids FX confusion for local buyers.

Owner:
Arjun owns the pricing page copy (INR), GST invoice note, and checkout test.

Risk:
Enterprise packaging is still undefined. Lena noted that larger customers may ask for SSO, admin roles, and invoice billing before committing to Team or Enterprise.

Open questions:
- Should the free plan include 3 projects or 5 projects?
- Should Team at ₹6,499 include priority support at launch?
